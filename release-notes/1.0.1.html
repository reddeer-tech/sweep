What's New in 1.0.1

Clear browser cookies and history

Browser History & Cookies now offers each browser's cookies, and its history older than an age you choose (30 days unless you change it), as separate rows that are never ticked for you. It works with Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Opera, Vivaldi, Firefox, Zen, LibreWolf, Waterfox and Safari's history, and only while that browser is closed

The cookies of sites you bookmarked, sites you visited in the last 7 days and sites you add in Settings ▸ Browser privacy are kept, all three are on until you turn them off. Passwords, autofill, bookmarks and open tabs are never touched, and Safari's cookies stay in Safari

Every clean keeps a copy for 7 days and Settings ▸ Browser privacy ▸ Undo puts it back. Firefox keeps its bookmarks in the same file as its history, so Firefox history is put back only if your bookmarks haven't changed since. Sweep deletes the copies the first time it is open after the 7 days, or straight away with Delete undo copies now

Empty folders

A new Empty Folders list shows folders in your home folder that are empty and haven't changed for 30 days. Nothing is ticked for you and anything you remove goes to the Trash

Scheduled cleaning, off until you turn it on

Settings ▸ Schedules can clean pure caches, Trash items older than 30 days, or browser cookies and history, weekly unless you pick daily or monthly. Every schedule is off until you switch it on, and nothing else is ever removed without your review

A schedule runs while Sweep is open, or Sweep can start itself at the time you pick. After each run Sweep tells you what it cleaned, that there was nothing to clean, or why it was skipped. The Trash and browser schedules need Full Disk Access, and the Trash on a drive that doesn't record when something was trashed, such as FAT32 or exFAT, is left alone

Clean

  • Each switch in Settings ▸ Scanning now covers every section that reads the same place, turning off external drives also leaves their Trash alone
  • Old logs inside sandboxed apps
  • Large & old files can also look in /Users/Shared and on connected drives, off until you turn it on
  • Duplicates can search up to 8 folders at once and show one type of file at a time
  • Select all or none within one section
  • The review page says which rows can be undone and how

Monitor, menu bar and updates

  • CPU and GPU temperatures, battery age, suggestions for what to clean next, more readings in the menu bar and all-time statistics
  • You can hide the menu bar icon
  • Turning off Check for updates automatically now really stops the automatic checks
  • Sweep now lets macOS rest it when idle and only stays busy while it scans, cleans or uninstalls

Uninstall

  • Filters for apps you haven't opened in months and for large apps
  • A background job or helper is shown but never ticked for you when it runs a program that isn't the app developer's, or code Sweep can't identify, or when Sweep can't see everything inside a folder
